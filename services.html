Questions

Before you book.

How do I book a session?

Send us a message through the contact page or WhatsApp with the occasion, your preferred date and the look you want. We’ll reply with ideas and a quote. A 70% deposit confirms your date.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices here are our starting rates. Every project is different, so prices are negotiable depending on what you need: tell us your plan and we’ll shape a package around it.

Do you travel for shoots and events?

Yes. We’re based at FUTA North Gate, Akure, and travel for weddings and events. For shoots outside Akure, transport and accommodation are added to the quote.

How does payment work?

70% upfront to secure the booking, and the remaining 30% when your final photos or film are delivered.

Will my photos have your logo on them?

No. Everything is delivered logo-free, so you can print, post and share it however you like.
